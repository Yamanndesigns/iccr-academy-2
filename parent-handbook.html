Parent Handbook

Policies, expectations, and everything families should know about the ICCRA school year.

School culture & expectations

ICCRA is built around a simple idea: academics and Islamic character formation belong together. The handbook lays out how that shows up day to day, from dress code and adab expectations to how we handle disagreements between students.

Academic policies

  • Attendance and make-up work for live sessions
  • Grading, progress reports, and parent-teacher communication
  • Technology use during live academic classes

Islamic studies policies

  • Expectations for in-person conduct at the Masjid
  • Qur'an memorization tracking and goals
  • Salah and daily practice during the school day

Health & safety

All in-person operations are held within the Masjid, supervised by Muslim administrators and staff. The handbook covers pickup/drop-off procedures, emergency contacts, and our approach to illness and safety.
