The Process

Five steps from first hello to first day

  1. 01

    Inquire

    Reach out to ICCRA administration to ask questions and confirm seat availability for your student's grade.

  2. 02

    Tour & meet

    Visit the Masjid campus, see a class in session, and meet the teachers who'll be working with your child.

  3. 03

    Apply

    Submit the enrollment application along with your student's academic records and any relevant documentation.

  4. 04

    Assessment & conversation

    A short placement conversation with faculty helps us understand your child's needs and confirm the right fit.

  5. 05

    Enroll & orient

    Complete registration and join a welcome orientation before your student's first day of class.
