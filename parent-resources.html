Getting started

New to ICCRA? These resources cover the basics families ask about most in their first few weeks — from login credentials for the academic portal to what to expect during Islamic studies time.

Attendance & scheduling

Questions about absences, late arrivals, or schedule changes should go through the Admin Coordinator, who can update records and notify the relevant teachers directly.

Support services

One-on-one tutoring in Math, Science, and ELA is available to any enrolled student — talk to your child's teacher or the Vice Principal to arrange a regular tutoring slot.
