School Calendar

Key dates for the current school year, plus a subscribable digital calendar that stays in sync automatically.

  • First day of school — late August
  • Fall break — mid-October
  • Winter break — late December through early January
  • Spring break — mid-March
  • Ramadan schedule adjustments — dates vary by lunar calendar
  • Last day of school — early June

Exact dates are confirmed each year and posted here as soon as they're finalized.
