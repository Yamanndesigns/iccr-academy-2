Volunteering Opportunities

ICCRA runs on the involvement of its community. Here's where extra hands make the biggest difference.

Classroom Support

Assist teachers with art, life-skills, and enrichment activities during the in-person Islamic studies block.

Tutoring

Support one-on-one or small-group tutoring in Math, Science, or ELA for students who want extra practice.

Event Support

Help set up and run community gatherings, iftars, field trips, and the end-of-year ceremony.

Administrative Help

Support the admissions and administration team with tours, paperwork, and family communication.
