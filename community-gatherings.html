Community Gatherings

ICCRA students grow up inside the life of the Masjid — these are the events that bring the whole community together.

Recurring gatherings

  • Ramadan programming, including nightly community iftars
  • Eid celebrations for students and families
  • Seasonal open houses for prospective families
  • End-of-year recognition and closing ceremony

How students take part

Older students often help organize and run these gatherings — setting up, greeting guests, and leading younger students — turning community events into genuine leadership opportunities.

Stay in the loop

Gathering dates are posted to the school calendar as they're confirmed, and announced through the ICCRA newsletter.
